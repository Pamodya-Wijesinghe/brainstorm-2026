About Brainstorm

Brainstorm is the signature event organized by the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society (EMBS) Student Branch Chapter of the University of Moratuwa.

It acts as a platform for young innovators, engineers, and medical enthusiasts to collaborate, propose, and build cutting-edge biomedical engineering solutions addressing real-world healthcare challenges.

The proposed solution can be either a physical product or a software application.
